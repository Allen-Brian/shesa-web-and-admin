## How to record and upload

1. **One file per numbered line.** Generate each line below as its own clip, named exactly as shown (for example `07-website.mp3`). MP3 is the expected format.
2. **Drop the files into `apps/web/public/platforms/vo/`.** You can also upload them here and I'll place them for you.
3. **That's all.** On load, the reel measures every clip and holds each scene for its line plus about a second, so the picture always waits for the voice. Missing clips fall back to a timed hold, so you can add them a few at a time.

### Voice direction

- **Tone:** warm, assured and unhurried, like a respected senior Shesan addressing peers and funders. Confident, never salesy.
- **Pace:** steady, with a clear beat after each full stop. The scenes reveal one point per sentence, so the pauses are where the pictures land.
- **Pronunciation:**
  - SHESA is "SHEH-sah"; Shesan(s) is "SHEH-san(s)".
  - SAHECO is "sah-HEH-ko".
  - "shesaconnect dot com" is written out in line 7 so the engine reads it as a URL.
  - MoMo is "MOH-moh".
- **Numbers** are written as words where the voice has to say them, for example "thirteen point seven five million". Leave them as written.
- **Music:** a bed plays underneath and dips automatically while the voice speaks. Optionally, put a track at `apps/web/public/platforms/bed.m4a`. Without one, the reel borrows the cup trailer's soundtrack.
